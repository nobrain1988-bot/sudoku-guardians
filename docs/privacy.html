Privacy Policy — Sudoku Guardians

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Sudoku Guardians is a sudoku puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. The app has no account system, no sign-up and no server of our own.

1. Information we collect

None. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. We cannot identify you.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally on your device so it is there when you come back. This never leaves your device and is not readable by us:

  • Your current puzzle, level, experience points and chosen companion
  • Which sudoku techniques you have learned
  • Daily Challenge streak and best times
  • Your settings — language, light or dark theme, sound and music on or off

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup we can restore.

3. Advertising

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process certain information from your device, including your advertising identifier, device type, operating system version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information and cannot link it to you.

Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices about ads

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's certified consent tool. You can decline, and you can change your choice later from the app's settings.
  • Rewarded ads — the ones that give you a hint explanation or bonus points — are never forced. You choose whether to watch one, and declining costs you nothing.

4. Purchases

If the app offers an optional purchase to remove ads, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, billing address or any payment information.

5. Children

Sudoku Guardians is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us and we will act on it.

6. Data we share

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. The only third party that receives data is Google, in its role as the advertising and app store provider described above.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, uninstall the app. To reset your advertising identifier, use your device settings as described in section 3.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: leejiwon44@nate.com

https://policies.google.com/privacy
